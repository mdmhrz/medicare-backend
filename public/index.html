Core Features

  • Authentication Email, password & Google OAuth with JWT sessions and role-based access control (Patient, Doctor, Admin).
  • Doctor Management Complete doctor profiles including specialties, qualifications, availability, ratings, and verification.
  • Appointment System Real-time booking & scheduling with flexible availability, status tracking, and automatic handling.
  • Prescriptions Digital prescription management with PDF generation and version history.
  • Reviews & Ratings Patient feedback system with 5-star ratings and automatic profile aggregation.
  • Payments Secure payment integration with Stripe for appointments and services.
